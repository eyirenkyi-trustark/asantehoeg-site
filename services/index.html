Monitoring, Evaluation & Data Development Services

Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) System Development

  • Design customized monitoring and evaluation frameworks aligned with funder requirements
  • Develop indicators, outcomes, and data collection plans that reflect real program impact
  • Support nonprofits in transitioning from ad-hoc tracking to structured, repeatable systems

Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for Data Collection & Analysis

  • Create clear, user-friendly SOPs for data collection, storage, and quality assurance
  • Establish roles, workflows, and timelines to reduce staff confusion and data gaps
  • Develop protocols for ethical, consistent, and reliable data practices

Data Analytics & Interpretation Support

  • Analyze program data to identify trends, outcomes, and areas for improvement
  • Translate raw data into actionable insights for leadership and program teams
  • Support organizations with limited in-house data capacity

Funder-Ready Reporting & Learning Tools

  • Develop reporting templates aligned with grant and funder expectations
  • Support quarterly, annual, and impact reporting processes
  • Translate data into clear narratives, visuals, and dashboards for funders and stakeholders

Capacity Building & Sustainability

  • Train staff on data collection tools, analysis basics, and reporting practices
  • Build internal capacity so systems remain functional beyond the engagement
  • Provide ongoing technical assistance as organizations grow

Training Services

Capacity Building Workshops

  • Monitoring, evaluation, and learning (MEL) — including feminist or participatory MEL.
  • Communication for social change or trauma-informed storytelling.

Train-the-Trainer Programs

  • Build cohorts of local facilitators equipped to deliver future trainings.
  • Focus on sustainable skill transfer and localized adaptation.

Online Learning Modules

  • Create self-paced, interactive learning modules on tools like Theory of Change, results frameworks, or narrative change.
  • Develop certification courses for NGOs or public-sector clients.

Organizational Learning Retreats

  • Facilitate strategic reflection or visioning retreats.
  • Integrate somatic, arts-based, or indigenous learning methods.

Program Design and Strategic Planning

Research Services

Applied Research for Social Impact

  • Design and implement community-based participatory research (CBPR).
  • Conduct baseline, midline, and endline studies to measure the impact of programs.

Organizational Diagnostics

  • Assess organizational culture, DEI practices, or power dynamics.
  • Research internal communication flow and effectiveness.

Knowledge Production

  • Develop white papers, think pieces, or policy briefs for advocacy.
  • Document best practices and lessons learned from NGO or social enterprise projects.

Market & Needs Assessments
