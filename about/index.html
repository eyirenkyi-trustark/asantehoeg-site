Meet The Team

Our diverse team of experts is committed to helping your organization thrive through data-driven insights.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower organizations with rigorous, research-driven insights that fuel bold, informed decision-making.

Through comprehensive data collection and analysis, we deliver clear, actionable strategies that sharpen outreach, deepen client understanding, and advance equity. We are committed to helping our partners grow with intention while guided by evidence, grounded in impact, and driven by values.

01

Vision

At Asante-Hoeg Consulting, we empower organizations to thrive through research-driven analysis. By first understanding constituents' needs, we help partners make informed decisions for impactful change.

02

Values

We prioritize diversity and inclusion as essential components of our vision. Through rigorous research-based analysis, we seek to cultivate strong community focused organizations and enhance their impact.

Leadership Team

Executive Director

Godfried Asante, PhD.

Dr. Asante is a researcher, educator, and consultant with a passion for helping organizations use data to advance equity, impact, and accountability. With a background in communication, participatory research, and monitoring & evaluation, he brings both academic rigor and practical insight to every project.

Godfried founded Asante-Hoeg Consulting to support small nonprofits and businesses that often lack in-house capacity to collect, analyze, and act on data. His approach is collaborative, justice-oriented, and deeply committed to making data usable and meaningful for real-world decision-making.

Joelle Cruz, PhD

Associate Consultant

Associate Consultant
